Right-click on the list header > Select View > Type “Location = California”> Select Run

Select the breadcrumb > Type “California” > Press Enter
Explanation

Correct answer

Right-click on the field with California value > Select Show Matching
Explanation

Right-clicking on the field with the California value and selecting Show Matching is the correct way to filter out results based on location. This action allows Max to specifically target the location field and display only the pets that live in California.

Question 60Correct

In which state must a knowledge article be to be visible to users?
Your answer is correct

Published
Explanation

Knowledge articles must be in the Published state to be visible to users. Published articles are approved, up-to-date, and accessible to users who need the information contained within them.

Pending
Explanation

There is no pending workflow state. The closest option to pending is draft and that represents articles that have been submitted and waiting to move forward in the approval process.

Retired
Explanation
Knowledge articles in the Retired state are no longer relevant or accurate and have been removed from the knowledge base. They are not visible to users as they are considered outdated.

Saved
Explanation
Knowledge articles in the Saved state are drafts that have been saved but not yet published. They are not visible to users until they are moved to the Published state.
